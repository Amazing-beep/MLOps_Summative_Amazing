Interpretation

  • Hard, imbalanced task: only ≈11% of encounters are 30-day readmissions, so accuracy is misleading; ROC-AUC (≈0.66) and recall are the headline metrics.
  • Cost-sensitive learning (scale_pos_weight=7.8) lifts recall from ≈1–2% (unweighted models) to ≈54%, at the price of lower precision (≈18%) — acceptable for a screening tool where a missed readmission is costlier than a false alarm.
  • Leakage control: patient-grouped splitting means no patient appears in more than one split, so test metrics are not inflated by repeat encounters.
  • Traditional ML vs deep learning: the best LightGBM and the best cost-sensitive deep MLP are statistically very close (test AUC 0.6566 vs 0.6574), so the lighter LightGBM is deployed — it trains in about a second and supports fast retraining.
